# Gap crosswalk onto the current rows (92bb279)

This page summarizes [`catalogs/landscape/gap-crosswalk-92bb279.json`](../catalogs/landscape/gap-crosswalk-92bb279.json). It maps the 40 gap receipts of [`gap-resolution-20260922`](gap-resolution-20260922.md) onto every `open_gaps` entry of the 32 landscape rows at `92bb279`, and gives each gap a blocker category and an owner for the next evidence wave. A status here says only whether an existing receipt addresses the gap. No verdict changes.

## Method

1. **Candidates.** Code pairs each gap with every receipt that names its layer: 835 pairs over 225 gaps.
2. **TypeSafe screen.** `jev-1.13.0` (question revision `gap-crosswalk-questions-v1`) gives each pair P(settles)+P(partially). A pair enters review at P ≥ 0.3. The [preregistration](../evidence/artifacts/gap-crosswalk-92bb279/PREREGISTRATION.md) fixed that rule before any call, and the eval on the 315 reviewed `bdd04ca` gaps chose the threshold: 27 of 29 addressed gaps were caught, with 50 false positives out of 150.
3. **Review.** Opus reviewers decided every queued pair and categorized every gap. A second, independent Opus reviewer re-checked each positive decision, and a disagreement replaces it. TypeSafe's own blocker categories agreed with the reviewed labels on 117 of 256 eval gaps, below the preregistered 0.60 bar, so they are kept only as `typesafe_blocker_advisory`.

## Limits

- **Indexes.** `index` refers to the rows at the source revision. A later re-record needs a new crosswalk.
- **Recall.** TypeSafe screens pairs, so a pair below the threshold was not reviewed. On the eval set this missed 2 of 29 addressed gaps.
- **Categories.** They are reviewer judgments under the stated operating context (one WSL2 workstation, paper orders only, no paid data, no second machine). They are not a guarantee that a check will succeed.
