Recommendations on repository evidence, not merit winners. Under program decision 5 and the accepted U11 design (revision 4, 89424e36), each layer's preregistered comparison on the new distribution selects its winner; a recommendation is one input to that comparison's arm set. Where both families agree, the recommendation stands as an arm; where they differ, both picks enter the comparison.

## Trading and cross-layer boundaries

The 12 us-equities layers own only trading-specific capabilities; shared infrastructure (agents, memory, scheduling, observability, storage tooling, CI, evaluation) is used from the owning foundation layer, not selected again. The trading lane owner accepted this rule on 2026-10-01 for its run after the GPT pool resets; its earlier trading selections that are now foundation-owned (Inspect AI, gitleaks, Grype, the observability stack) become uses of the foundation layers.

- analytical storage (DuckDB with Parquet): us-equities storage-compute. its requirement names exact reproducible analytical snapshots; no foundation requirement names analytical storage
- research data versioning and pipeline reproduction (DVC): us-equities identity-provenance. the same requirement text; Restic (recovery-portability) backs up and restores host and application state, a different job
- research experiment tracking (MLflow): us-equities evaluation-experiments. research runs; evaluations, including model-route qualification, belong to Inspect AI in quality-evaluation
- market-data validation (pandera): us-equities data-quality-orchestration. trading-specific on any reading (the trading lane owner)
- retrieval evaluation (agent-retrieval-bench): foundation quality-evaluation (the harness); a candidate task set for the semantic-rag and document-retrieval comparisons. evaluation is a foundation job; admitted as a named external benchmark with a recorded reason when those comparisons are preregistered
