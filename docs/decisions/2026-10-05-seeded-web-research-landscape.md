## Source corrections and qualification boundaries

Exa's external benchmark is a discovery lead, not proof of the anonymous MCP arm.
The pinned judge accepts a retrieved URL **or** a retrieved gold token, and its URL
match permits prefixes. It therefore does not prove strict claim/source support.
The proposed local comparison must specify its own attribution oracle and retain
both returned sources and independently checked claims. These points follow
[judge.py@a4fc6411:89](https://github.com/openbenchmarks-labs/web-search-for-coding-agents/blob/a4fc6411013b504385d035fa74f57fab9fd83940/scripts/coding_search/judge.py#L89)
and [:195](https://github.com/openbenchmarks-labs/web-search-for-coding-agents/blob/a4fc6411013b504385d035fa74f57fab9fd83940/scripts/coding_search/judge.py#L195).
[Exa's pinned search tool:68](https://github.com/exa-labs/exa-mcp-server/blob/f3d71fb6b0ff4b4683f108f05bc2bae61a9f7e97/src/tools/webSearch.ts#L68)
defaults its configured search type to `auto`; transfer from a different search
mode is unmeasured. A source commit cannot freeze a changing hosted service.

The LettuceDetect fit vote reports finding no configured promptfoo
context-faithfulness use in the reviewed checkout. That observation is narrower than
assertion availability and does not establish a current installed-client absence.
Retain it as reviewer evidence at `returns.json#/votes/web-research/8/fit`, not an
accepted citation-check deployment. The prospective experiment must balance recall
with precision/false acceptance and preserve long-source/chunking cases.

CDX payload equality cannot be tested using newline normalization alone. The
published [REDACTIONS.json](../../evidence/artifacts/gap-wave2-20260923/us-equities__market-data-reference/raw/REDACTIONS.json)
retains original hashes and describes redactions and ICE HTML-to-text publication.
The recorded facts vote also warns that the published NYSE subset is incomplete.
Use original hashes/CDX digests or the full declared publication transformation;
do not compare transformed artifacts with raw downloads and label the difference
a retrieval failure. This publication did not read private originals or fetch
archive payloads.

Browser-use and Playwright MCP were seed leads, with Claude `not_adopted` proposals
retained under `returns.json#/raw/web-research/first/claude_discover/proposed/5`
and `/3`, then dropped at discovery (`#/raw/web-research/first/dropped`). They are
not two of the eleven formal refutations. Keep the raw discovery and cap evidence;
do not manufacture missing votes or rewrite the original first-round critic.
