## Separate gates after this handoff

Historical identity, delistings, corporate actions, source revisions and eligible
universe coverage still gate strategy claims. Native in-flight ambiguous-submit,
partial-fill/cancel, disconnect/restart and stream/snapshot recovery remain
separate from the Alpaca full-fill/completed-trial observation. IBKR requires its
own native paper identity, socket, contract, data and risk acceptance. Continuous
operation, service identity, alerts and restart/reboot recovery require their own
observations. No foundation benchmark or reviewer agreement closes these gates.

Use the existing native commands and dashboards selected by the task. Preserve
model/account/permission settings, give writers isolated checkouts, and keep
numeric execution/risk rules in code. Reopen foundation selection when a measured
task gap requires it. Refresh the native lifetime report after meaningful
efficiency changes; estimates, artifact comparisons and provider usage are
different quantities and must not be added together.
