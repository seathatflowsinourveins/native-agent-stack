## Why this discovery pass stops here

The examined layers now have explicit defaults, alternatives, source pins and
acceptance gates. Additional orchestration/memory/trace frameworks would mostly
duplicate selected responsibilities. Fresh model metadata supplies no measured
replacement advantage. This is bounded convergence of the examined choices,
not proof that the landscape contains no other useful repository. Discovery did
find new candidates and evidence gaps; the word “saturated” must not hide those.

found the 337 public-star identities unchanged. This is reproducible dependency
