VERDICT: needs_changes

1. **The calendar comparison already exists.** I verified hashes for retained results comparing exchange_calendars 4.13.2 with pandas_market_calendars 5.4.0: **3,016 sessions across 2016–2027**, 24 early closes, and no XNYS-versus-NYSE session or opening/closing-time disagreements. The proposal’s “never run side by side” claim is false. Reuse this historical evidence. Also specify PMC’s native `NYSE` calendar: its `XNYS` implementation mirrors exchange_calendars, weakening independence. [Retained results](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/seathatflowsinourveins/native-agent-stack/720e294b5c10211d8f75a939bc478af734279d74/evidence/artifacts/gap-wave2-20260923/us-equities__market-data-reference/raw/6-calendar-check.fix1.json), [upstream mirror implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsheftel/pandas_market_calendars/v5.4.0/pandas_market_calendars/calendars/mirror.py).

2. **Calendar paper-lane acceptance is overstated.** The fallback applies to years outside the frozen tables. Its own source explicitly identifies it as untested and unverified in the recorded environment, where exchange_calendars was not installed. An import path does not support “paper acts passed.” [Pinned session implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/seathatflowsinourveins/native-agent-stack/720e294b5c10211d8f75a939bc478af734279d74/blueprints/us-equities/adaptive-paper/sessions.py).

3. **FedFred’s rejection is factually wrong.** Its documentation exposes `get_series_vintagedates()` and observation parameters `realtime_start`, `realtime_end`, and `vintage_dates`. The Federal Reserve explicitly documents historical real-time periods as ALFRED functionality. Restore FedFred as a macro-vintage trial candidate; actual correctness remains untested here. [FedFred observations](https://nikhilxsunder.github.io/fedfred/api/_autosummary/fedfred.FredAPI.get_series_observations.html), [Federal Reserve semantics](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html).

4. **WRDS restrictions do not justify rejecting CRSP generally.** CRSP documents direct MOVEit delivery, Windows/Linux support, and SAS/ASCII/R files, alongside third-party access. Its current Morningstar page explicitly includes investment practitioners among licensees. Reclassify direct CRSP access as requiring entitlement and license evaluation, rather than an established licensing prohibition. [CRSP delivery options](https://www.crsp.org/research__trashed/crsp-us-stock-databases/), [current product and licensee description](https://indexes.morningstar.com/research-data-products/crsp-us-stock-databases).

5. **Massive has a concrete advantage relevant to an identified gap.** Its dividend endpoint explicitly returns dividend-payment `currency`. Its paid tiers advertise 10 or 20+ years of history; rejecting it solely against the free tier while admitting paid Databento and Sharadar trials applies inconsistent criteria. Restore a conditional corporate-action comparison, with coverage and rights still unverified. [Dividend schema](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/corporate-actions/dividends), [pricing and history](https://massive.com/pricing).

6. **The EdgarTools 13F failure is exaggerated, and the proposed fixture is unsuitable.** v5.59.1 fixes the `edgar_read` holdings presentation; the repaired output contains the top 30 securities. That does not establish failure of the full Python holdings parser. Moreover, the suggested repository fixture contains **8-K/8-K/A filings**, not 13F holdings. Compare a real 13F-HR information table with normalized units and aggregation. The SEC’s own downloadable 13F datasets are an omitted free cross-check. [Release notes](https://github.com/dgunning/edgartools/releases/tag/v5.59.1), [v5.58.0 holdings API](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dgunning/edgartools/v5.58.0/docs/guides/thirteenf-data-object-guide.md), [fixture](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/seathatflowsinourveins/native-agent-stack/720e294b5c10211d8f75a939bc478af734279d74/blueprints/us-equities/catalyst-provenance/access-resolution.json), [SEC datasets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-13f-data-sets).

7. **Databento’s trial does not establish point-in-time correctness.** SDK v0.87.0 defaults to `index="event_date"` and `pit=False`; the latter collapses revisions using `groupby(...).agg("last")`. Repeated byte-identical pulls can therefore reproduce a stable, hindsight-contaminated result. Require retained revisions, explicit knowledge-time cutoffs, raw records, and observation timestamps. `pit=True` preserves revisions but does not itself enforce the decision cutoff. [Pinned corporate-actions implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/databento/databento-python/v0.87.0/databento/reference/api/corporate.py).

8. **Sharadar needs separate capability tests.** AR fundamentals are documented, but filing-date indexing does not establish intraday availability or complete vendor revision history. Matching EdgarTools’ values should count as correctness evidence; it should not automatically reject Sharadar’s standardized coverage. Evaluate fundamentals and dividend currency separately. Also, an old package release alone does not prove the Nasdaq client unusable—the official repository still documents it as the supported Python client. [Sharadar dimensions and limitations](https://sharadar.com/docs/fundamentals), [Nasdaq client documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nasdaq/data-link-python/main/README.md).

9. **Norgate’s rejection survives, with stronger evidence and a factual correction.** It has a documented Python API, including historical constituent queries. However, its actual EULA §21 requires deletion of subscription Content and Information after expiration, while permitting retention of Derived Data. That directly matters to retained-source reproducibility; cite this instead of leaving the EULA unread. [Python API](https://pypi.org/project/norgatedata/), [EULA](https://norgatedata.com/subscribe/eula.php).

10. **Tick Data is a strong omitted candidate.** Its documented offering includes U.S. history since 1993, tick/minute data, inactive securities, historical symbol mapping, and corporate actions. This warrants comparison for historical mover discovery. These remain vendor claims requiring acceptance; notably, TickAPI’s leased-data terms require an active subscription, so retention rights need evaluation. [U.S. equities offering](https://www.tickdata.com/product/us-equities/), [TickAPI terms](https://www.tickdata.com/data-delivery/tickapi).

11. **Two other rejection descriptions need correction.** OpenBB’s pinned license explicitly declares AGPLv3; GitHub’s `NOASSERTION` does not make that declaration ambiguous. Deferral for insufficient added capability remains reasonable. FinanceDatabase explicitly retains delisted tickers, contradicting the current-snapshot characterization, although that still does not establish dated universe membership. [OpenBB license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBB-finance/OpenBB/3e071fcc/LICENSE), [FinanceDatabase documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JerBouma/FinanceDatabase/main/README.md).

12. **Release freshness mostly checks out; comparative superiority does not.** Alpaca-py 0.44.0 and exchange_calendars 4.13.2 remain current; EdgarTools 5.58.0 is behind 5.59.1. Retaining these tools as scoped baselines is defensible. Successful acquisition volume and the absence of executed challenger arms do not establish that alternatives cannot outperform them. Keep the upgrade proposal subject to focused compatibility acceptance. [Alpaca PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/alpaca-py/), [exchange_calendars PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/exchange_calendars/), [EdgarTools release](https://github.com/dgunning/edgartools/releases/tag/v5.59.1).
