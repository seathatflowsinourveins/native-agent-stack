The proposal needs corrections before acceptance. I treated verification entries after the relay cutoff as undelivered.

1. **Temporal and DBOS do not guarantee zero repeated external calls.** Temporal may rerun an activity after its external effect succeeds but before completion is recorded. DBOS explicitly gives steps at-least-once guarantees. Rewrite both trials to distinguish repeated calls from duplicate effects, require idempotency or reconciliation, and inject failure between the effect and its checkpoint. [Temporal semantics](https://docs.temporal.io/activity-definition#idempotency), [DBOS guarantees](https://docs.dbos.dev/explanations/concurrent-executions).

2. **The Dagu challenge understates the accepted baseline.** Its pinned 2.16.6 implementation supports selected-step retry. Moreover, the proposal’s cited [qualification receipt](evidence/artifacts/sota-refresh-20260923/pins-runtime/dagu.json:42) records that recovery passing on **2.17.0**, with the completed checkpoint executed once. Automatic recovery after SIGKILL remains inconclusive; recoverable state generally is not untested. Keep `challenged` specific to that unresolved mechanism. [Pinned Dagu retry implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dagucloud/dagu/v2.16.6/internal/cmd/retry.go).

3. **The Dagster trial can pass while losing fail-closed behavior.** Attaching an asset check does not, by default, prevent downstream materialization when it fails. Require `blocking=True`, appropriate failure severity, and negative fixtures proving downstream promotion never executes. Reproducing 12 passing checks plus lineage is insufficient. [Dagster blocking semantics](https://docs.dagster.io/guides/test/asset-checks#blocking-downstream-materialization).

4. **The orchestration shortlist applies inconsistent criteria.** Prefect’s persisted task results and retries address recoverable work; Airflow explicitly detects abandoned running tasks and fails or retries them. Their operational cost can justify deferral, but “no relevant recovery gap” cannot. Conversely, Pueue documents persisted queues/logs and command restart, which does not establish interrupted-step recovery. Its proposed history-restoration test would not demonstrate the claimed advantage. [Prefect caching](https://docs.prefect.io/v3/concepts/caching), [Airflow heartbeat recovery](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/stable/core-concepts/tasks.html), [Pueue capabilities](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nukesor/pueue/main/README.md).

5. **Pandera’s evidence supports a bounded retention decision, with omitted limitations.** Version 0.33.1 checks out. However, the real 480-row trial ran on September 23 from `843dc8f`; its snapshot CSV was not retained, and its gate differs from the current file. The cited `good-gate-result.json` contains only four synthetic rows. Consequently, the proposed comparison cannot simply reuse that real snapshot. Also replace GE “already ran this exact comparison” with “source-reviewed; comparison unexecuted.” [Pandera release](https://pypi.org/project/pandera/0.33.1/), [actual trial](blueprints/us-equities/adaptive-paper/trials/20260923g-main-passed/README.md:1), [retention limitation](blueprints/us-equities/data/README.md:185).

6. **Pointblank is a strong missing data-quality challenger.** It directly supports Pandas, Polars and DuckDB, validation reports, and assertions that stop execution on failed checks. This deserves a matched comparison before calling GE the strongest challenger: identical defect rules, false accepts/rejects, execution cost and dependency burden. Its capabilities establish relevance, not superiority. [Pointblank repository](https://github.com/posit-dev/pointblank), [fail-closed assertions](https://posit-dev.github.io/pointblank/user-guide/advanced-validation/assertions.html).

7. **Restate is a missing durable-runtime challenger.** Its Python SDK and journaled recovery of completed steps directly address the proposed durability comparison. Register it for the same failure experiment, preserving external-effect idempotency requirements. Account for its BUSL-1.1 license rather than treating it as an Apache-licensed substitute. [Restate capabilities](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/restatedev/restate/main/README.md), [license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/restatedev/restate/main/LICENSE).

8. **The dbt version “ambiguity” is resolvable from upstream.** The repository explicitly explains that `main` contains the Rust v2 implementation, while Python v1 development lives on `1.latest`. Calling it a meta-repository leaves an avoidable factual error. Its deferral for the present workload may stand, but identify the engine and distribution correctly. [Upstream explanation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dbt-labs/dbt/main/README.md).
