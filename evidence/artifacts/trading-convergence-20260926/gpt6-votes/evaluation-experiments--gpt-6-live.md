1. **FinSearchComp and FinMCP-Bench are not “paper only.”** FinSearchComp releases its dataset, agent runner, and `eval.py`. FinMCP-Bench releases benchmark data, inference examples, and an executable tool-call evaluator. Reclassify both as available evaluation assets requiring qualification. The truncated verification tail was treated as undelivered; these findings come from independent source inspection. [FinSearchComp](https://github.com/randomtutu/FinSearchComp), [FinMCP-Bench implementation](https://github.com/aliyun/qwen-dianjin/tree/master/DianJin-TIR), [evaluator source](https://github.com/aliyun/qwen-dianjin/blob/master/DianJin-TIR/eval/evaluation.py).

2. **The other two “paper only” rejections also fail.** Backtrader-Bench provides executable question generation/checking, datasets, runners, and installation instructions. Temporal Leakage provides analysis code, data, and experiment results, although its README identifies unreleased twin checkpoints. Availability does not establish complete reproducibility or local acceptance, but the stated rejection reason is false. [Backtrader-Bench](https://github.com/rzhao999/Backtrader-Bench), [Temporal Leakage reproducibility package](https://github.com/ZeyuZhang1901/Temporal-Leakage-Backtesting).

3. **MTEB is materially mischaracterized.** It evaluates retrieval systems, not merely embeddings/STS. The local STSBenchmark smoke test cannot define the toolkit’s full scope. ARB remains credible for code-context retrieval, but financial retrieval deserves a separate comparison. BEIR supplies retrieval evaluation and FiQA; the omitted FinanceBench supplies 150 public financial-QA examples with answers and evidence—not the entire 10,231-question collection. [MTEB](https://github.com/embeddings-benchmark/mteb), [BEIR](https://github.com/beir-cellar/beir), [FinanceBench](https://github.com/patronus-ai/financebench).

4. **Phoenix’s rejection understates its capabilities and misstates the relevant license restriction.** Even the reviewed `20.14.0` README lists response/retrieval evaluation, versioned datasets, experiments, and prompt management. ELv2’s cited restriction concerns providing substantial functionality to third parties as a hosted service; describing that as a general “self-host restriction” is misleading. Conditional retention is defensible, but the narrower-than-evaluation characterization needs correction. [Pinned README](https://github.com/Arize-ai/phoenix/blob/arize-phoenix-v20.14.0/README.md), [license](https://github.com/Arize-ai/phoenix/blob/main/LICENSE).

5. **DeepEval is a strong omitted candidate for investigation.** It supplies explicit MCP-use, MCP-task-completion, and conversational evaluation capabilities. A bounded comparison using identical recorded tool trajectories would directly address this stack’s evaluation needs. Its documented judge-based metrics need calibration; their existence does not establish superiority over Inspect. [DeepEval](https://github.com/confident-ai/deepeval), [MCP-use metric](https://deepeval.com/docs/metrics-mcp-use), [MCP-task-completion metric](https://deepeval.com/docs/metrics-mcp-task-completion).

6. **The validation-tool exclusions apply an unnecessarily narrow scope.** Lacuna explicitly describes itself as a validation library rather than a backtester, with experiment history and reproducible reports. Falsification-ledger implements preregistration and evidence contracts. Those overlap “evaluation and experiments,” although neither has earned adoption here. Low stars, youth, or synchronized repository pushes are insufficient substitutes for capability and reproducibility assessment. [Lacuna](https://github.com/eyenoticeall/Lacuna), [falsification-ledger](https://github.com/holdout-labs/falsification-ledger).

7. **River’s “no predictive edge” conclusion exceeds its evidence.** The retained delayed model loses on accuracy, **0.592 versus 0.612**, but improves LogLoss, **0.681 versus 0.764**. Neither establishes a statistically reliable trading advantage; the results support a negative accuracy finding, not an unqualified absence of predictive information. Furthermore, a model’s performance does not determine the usefulness of its evaluator. Keeping River conditional on an actual streaming requirement remains reasonable. [Recorded results](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/native-agent-stack/blob/main/evidence/artifacts/gap-wave2-20260923/us-equities__evaluation-experiments/7-river-delayed-progressive-validation.json), [upstream delayed-validation implementation](https://github.com/online-ml/river/blob/main/river/evaluate/progressive_validation.py).

It does not test Promptfoo’s native Claude-session provider or skill-use assertions. Preserve the bounded comparison result and leave those capabilities conditional. [Matched comparison](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/native-agent-stack/blob/main/evidence/artifacts/gap-wave2-20260923/us-equities__evaluation-experiments/2-matched-promptfoo-inspect-comparison.json), [native-provider documentation](https://www.promptfoo.dev/docs/providers/claude-agent-sdk/).
