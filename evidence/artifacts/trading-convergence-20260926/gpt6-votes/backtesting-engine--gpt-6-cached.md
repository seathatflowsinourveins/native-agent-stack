2. **VectorBT’s rejection contains two factual errors.** It supports portfolios spanning multiple instruments with shared capital through `cash_sharing=True`. Rust acceleration is also available to the public package through `vectorbt[rust]`; it is not exclusively a PRO capability. Broker-execution requirements may still exclude it as the destination engine, but these arguments cannot. Sources: [portfolio grouping](https://vectorbt.dev/api/portfolio/base/#grouping), [version 1.1.1](https://pypi.org/project/vectorbt/1.1.1/), [Rust engine](https://pypi.org/project/vectorbt-rust/).

3. **Lumibot merits a challenger trial.** Its primary sources document IBKR and Alpaca integrations; its backtester implements market-order fills at bar open and dividend cash credits. These mechanisms directly concern the proposal’s stated comparison criteria. An awesome-list comment about slowness is neither a measured performance result nor sufficient rejection evidence. Add a bounded comparison of identical inputs, fill timing/prices, dividend cash, fees and runtime. These source findings establish trial relevance, not acceptance. Sources: [IBKR](https://lumibot.lumiwealth.com/brokers.interactive_brokers.html), [Alpaca](https://lumibot.lumiwealth.com/brokers.alpaca.html), [fill implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lumiwealth/lumibot/master/lumibot/backtesting/backtesting_broker.py), [dividend implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lumiwealth/lumibot/master/lumibot/strategies/_strategy.py).
