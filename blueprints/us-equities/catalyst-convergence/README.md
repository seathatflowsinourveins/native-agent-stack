# Catalyst-readiness convergence

This wave advances three concrete capabilities: documentary security lifecycle
qualification, a frozen daily/intraday catalyst experiment, and a bounded native
research-task efficiency comparison. The [plan](plan.json) records the accepted
scope. Existing [identity observation evidence](../identity-readiness/README.md)
remains unchanged.

## Lifecycle sources

The fixed public acquisition captured six documents with six HTTP200 responses:
Meta's ticker-change announcement, Roundhill's issuer-distributed ticker-change
announcement, and Twitter's SEC8-K and25-NSE submissions with their index pages.
There were no new broker or price-history requests. The source bundle preserves
exact response bodies, status, prepared URLs, observation times and hashes in
private storage. Public evidence contains bounded summaries and integrity anchors.

The small [capture adapter](capture_sources.py) uses native Requests2.34.2. It
freezes the six-source plan and transport identity, disables redirects, retries,
environment proxies and netrc, limits each body to8MiB, and stops requests to an
origin after an access refusal. The configured contact identity is sent only to
the SEC. Raw capture directories must be outside Git. Independent review and
seven focused transport/privacy checks passed before acquisition.

The [offline lifecycle qualifier](../lifecycle-sample/README.md) uses native
EdgarTools and DuckDB. Its two views must remain distinct: documents can support
retrospective event claims, while local observation-time selection shows when
this runtime received those documents. Company, fund, share class, ticker and
permanent security identity are different fields. Ticker reuse does not explain
the quarantined bar from the prior wave. Suspension and a delisting notice do
not establish an exact legal delisting instant or a complete historical universe.

## Frozen experiment

The [daily/intraday protocol](../catalyst-experiment/README.md) defines two
exploratory hypotheses, candidate eligibility, source timing, entry latency,
exits, costs, controls, chronological partitions and research promotion criteria.
It distinguishes each +200% outcome definition and preserves inspected samples.
Its thresholds were not selected using newly fetched returns.

Native DuckDB verified five synthetic observations at four cutoffs, selecting
0/1/1/3 rows. A future-source perturbation did not change earlier selection, and
BIGINT arithmetic retained one nanosecond at modern epoch magnitudes. Thirteen
focused checks passed. Independent review corrected an exact +200% boundary bug;
both the initial and corrected synthetic runs are retained. These results establish
mechanics, not actual feature construction, portfolio accounting or strategy merit.

## Native research efficiency

The [comparison](../research-efficiency/README.md) uses a frozen public corpus,
two questions, native client sessions and counterbalanced full-versus-selected
context conditions. Retrieval queries and quality criteria are fixed before
inference. Missing retrieval evidence, refusals, timeouts and unexpected tools
remain outcomes. Native configuration and model identity are recorded.

Quality review precedes interpretation of usage. Provider counters, cached-token
subsets, reasoning subsets, local text reductions and complete task totals are
separate quantities. This comparison excludes the coordinator and study-design
work; it cannot establish the total cost or savings of this broader goal.

Eight native invocations completed with no repeat inference. Both blinded reviewers
accepted semantic quality at9.5–10/10. The separate250word contract rejected both
Claude full-context answers; their tokens remain in the comparison.

## Catalog and continuation

The [native public-star refresh](public-stars-refresh.json) found342 unique
identities over four pages, with no additions, removals or renames against the
previous snapshot. Existing source reviews retain their own dates; a metadata
refresh does not repeat native acceptance or make an uninstalled repository active.
Concurrent reviewed GitHub updates were merged and preserved. The combined
catalog now records504 identities:342 starred and162 beyond, with1040 typed
references after this wave's three retained-component decisions. No new repository
was installed by this wave. Its purpose was acceptance of selected capabilities.

The next empirical gate remains an eligible historical sample with supported
identity, coverage and availability assumptions. A complete market universe,
original provider revisions, realistic fills, paper recovery and operational
off-host hosting remain separate acceptance requirements. Each accepted result
updates the catalog, dashboard and portable continuation through reviewed evidence.

The next dependent move is a bounded historical candidate-table design that labels
retrospective assumptions explicitly and includes eligible non-movers and failures.
Original source availability, identity and inactive coverage must be either supported
or kept outside an as-known evaluation. Use the frozen daily/intraday protocol and
inspection registry before opening new returns; then implement deterministic feature,
cost and fill accounting. The next efficiency study needs unseen questions and a
fixed concise output contract, with all failed conditions retained. Current native
sign-ins worked; no interactive login was needed for this wave.
