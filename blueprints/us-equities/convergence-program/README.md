# Convergence program and next roles

This goal continues the dated architecture through evidence-driven research,
native acceptance and publication. The [structured plan](plan.json) defines
ownership, requirements, quality rules and stopping criteria. It does not claim
a universal final ranking or automatically enable trading, paid hosting or feeds.

One coordinator and three bounded workers cover foundation/memory, trading/data,
and hosting/operations. Writing workers use separate worktrees; independent review
precedes integration. Models review small source-linked packets; deterministic
code owns data arithmetic, validation, process policy, risk and order state.

The new native acceptances address disposable memory lifecycle and scope,
exact nanosecond replay, and whole-worker-tree supervision. Source reviews
also challenge current choices across all seventeen mapped requirements, including
macOS/WSL, community/awesome-list discovery and the Alpaca north star.

Complete identity coverage is separate from source/code review. A new model,
large star count or awesome-list entry is not sufficient to replace an accepted
component. Each promotion needs a concrete advantage, compatible native workflow
and explicit license, dependency, platform and evidence boundaries.

Each wave ends with selected defaults, alternatives, missing requirements and
exact command/results. Convergence applies to the examined requirements and
candidates; another meaningful challenger reopens its affected decision. Real
data entitlement, risk criteria, another host and broker progression retain
their own acceptance gates.

The selected research direction remains [daily/intraday catalysts and historical
extreme movers](../acceptance-wave/research-protocol.md), with [factor inputs and
future market-state selection](../acceptance-wave/factors-regimes.md). Model or
memory suggestions cannot silently promote a strategy into the active pool.

The [requirement coverage map](coverage.md) links current defaults and remaining
gates. The [native Astra/Claude review](review-adjudication.md) records direct
workflow results and **37,151 reported tokens**, with interpretive corrections.
The [grand dashboard](../../../observability/grand-dashboard/README.md) exposes
live telemetry alongside recorded experiment, decision, gate and worker state.
Historical simulation is the current execution priority; paper credentials will
be configured locally later by the user.

The [new historical simulation](../historical-simulation/README.md) completed six
frozen SPY hourly scenarios across 104 sessions. From USD 100,000, stressed-cost
1x ended at 90,358.00, 2x at 75,607.75 (three native margin liquidations), and the
fixed causal exposure reduction at 95,324.10. Every invested case lost money in
this selected stress window. Over-limit buying power was rejected without a fill.
This is engineering acceptance, not a profitable strategy or holdout evaluation.

The next useful work is an entitled historical/PIT universe and all-candidate
catalyst study with negative controls, frozen definitions, realistic funding and
execution assumptions, and held-out evaluation. The existing fixed stress cases
should not be repeated to inflate evidence counts or tuned into a claimed edge.
