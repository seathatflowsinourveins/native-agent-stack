The source proposal originally binds shared, coding and evaluation contracts.
The gatherer binding above was independently read from the unchanged prior file
for this affected-job amendment. Historical task/question/term records, failures
and their original conditions remain under their prior hashes; this proposal

`full_usage` retains every original complete-aggregate, field/operand, coverage,
efficiency and decision requirement. Its existing quality and efficiency
thresholds are unchanged. An unknown usage operand cannot fall through a usage
tiebreak to time. The new mode is isolated by explicit applicability fields in
the shared usage rules and affected job prerequisites and selection rules.

`quality_only` removes complete-provider usage and original efficiency operands
as prerequisites for a useful-task quality decision. It still requires a complete
planned/emitted attempt and outcome inventory, including main/child/helper/grader
calls, internal retries, interruptions, invalid blocks and recoveries. Keep native
reward/value, status, reason, exception and failure disposition before applying
the frozen oracle. Native summary replacement or a numeric partial total does not
prove exhaustive attempts. Actual runtime/client/route/model/effort/parallel and
helper/grader identity, native orchestration, request-to-attempt binding and
gateway intervention evidence must be observed independently of counter
availability.

Preserve each original counter's presence/type/value and explicitly identified
source, attempts, roles and coverage. A partial counter may be reported in its
observed scope; a complete-provider aggregate remains null unless complete
coverage is established. Unknown or absent usage is never zero, an estimate,
extrapolation or a complete total. Preserve native normalized values separately
without reconstructing missing original operands. Cost/billing, efficiency,
savings and token-feature adoption remain HOLD in `quality_only`. Neither usage,
time nor resource footprint ranks a quality tie; retain the relevant incumbent.

The affected quality rules are prospectively declared as follows:

- Coding retains all 24 tasks and two valid repeats per arm: 48 task-repeat
  blocks, 144 planned arm attempts and 0–48 successful valid trials per arm.
  Native success counts are 0–2 per task. A challenger must lose no task count
  and improve at least one by at least 1 over OpenHands1.50.1. Rank qualified
  challengers by total successful valid trials; equal greatest totals or a
  baseline quality tie retain OpenHands. No unknown-token or time tiebreak is
  available. Infrastructure-invalid quality stays null; native failures score 0
  only under the unchanged frozen disposition. Retain all invalid/recovery and
  helper/grading attempts with no selective exclusion.
- Gathering retains eight questions and two repeats per arm: 16 task-repeat
  blocks and 48 planned reports. Use the unchanged standing Inspect0.3.273
  `model_graded_qa` binary C/I contract and frozen strict support criterion,
  alongside the unchanged official DRB-II rubric. Native UNSCORED/errors still
  invalidate the whole block; they cannot shrink its denominator. Onyx must lose
  neither strict-supported report count nor mean official rubric score on any
  question and improve at least one count by 1 or one mean strictly above
  DeerFlow. The official numeric field, complete original rubric coverage and
  failure dispositions must be bound before outputs. Preserve original rubric
  item/dimension/blocked-rate values separately; rubric score is not citation
  support. Equal quality retains DeerFlow2.1.0; GPTR3.7.0 remains the fixed
  reference. No scorer, default instructions/pattern, required grader role,
  partial-credit setting, strict criterion, parse/coverage acceptance or negative
  control is relaxed. The grader binding's efficiency/accounting prerequisites
  apply to `full_usage`; original field missingness/scope and actual grader
  identity remain required in both modes.
- Evaluation retains separate Inspect0.3.273 model/task and Harbor0.23.0
  containerized-agent incumbents. A useful-task comparison needs common native
  tasks/input hashes/formats and an unchanged maintained oracle for each subjob,
  two repeats and complete correctly classified outcomes/artifact recovery.
  Before outputs, bind a numeric quality metric, positive numeric minimum gain,
  comparison direction, all challenger ordering and native failure dispositions.
  The numeric gain and challenger order remain null and block freeze until bound.
  Quality ties or equal greatest qualified gains retain the corresponding
  incumbent. Different readiness fixture suites, aggregate pass counts or process
  exit establish no evaluator winner or candidate promotion.
